The stake behind every verdict.

A watcher's opinion is worth exactly what they put behind it. $REVE is what they put behind it.

There is no token at this time. The design below is what $REVE will do at launch.

Follow for updates How watchers work

One token, three duties.

01

It buys the right to vote.

A watcher stakes $REVE before it can rule on anything. There is no reputation score and no allowlist - the stake is the entire credential.

02

It pays for correct calls.

Verify honestly and you take a cut of every profit your reviews cleared, distributed weekly.

03

It burns for wrong ones.

Approve calldata that drains a vault and your stake is slashed and burned. It is not redistributed, so nobody profits from your mistake.

Where the fees go.

Gross profit is split onchain the moment a strategy settles - nothing accrues off-ledger.
